## The funnel (how money actually flows)

```
   CONTENT (from the dataset — you already own it)
   Reels · carousels · app screen-records
        │
        ▼
   REACH → FOLLOWERS            ← the asset being built
        │
        ├──────────────┬──────────────────────┐
        ▼              ▼                      ▼
   STAGE 1 (now)   STAGE 2 (~10–20k)     STAGE 3 (always)
   App installs    Sponsored posts       The account itself
   → $3.99 IAP     NYC restaurants,      = independent asset,
   (slow money:    tours, events pay     de-risks the app
   100k views ≈    local accounts
   ~15 sales)      early
```

**Honest expectation:** Stage 1 barely pays. The compounding audience is the
real return; stages 2–3 are where profit lives.

## What to post (ranked by leverage)

| Format | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Reel — routed day** | "A perfect rainy Saturday in the Village — routed" | The itinerary is the moat; no food account can copy it |
| **Reel — 3 spots** | "3 Chinatown spots locals actually go" | Fast to batch from the dataset; broad reach |
| **App screen-record** | Plan generated → map routes the day → real footage | Sells the product doing its trick |
| **Carousel — day plan** | Map → stops → dishes, 6–8 slides | Saves > likes; saves drive reach |
| **Official-account photos** | Same 20–30 images as the in-app seed feed | One shoot, two platforms |

## The growth mechanic

**Comment-to-DM loop:** caption every route Reel with
"Comment DUMBO and I'll send the full route" → auto-DM tool (ManyChat or
similar) replies with the route + App Store link.
Comments feed the algorithm · DMs are warm install prospects · zero ad spend.

Plus: always tag the location · collab with NYC micro-creators (offer
founding-member codes) · never buy followers.

## Cadence (solo-dev honest)

- **3 Reels/week**, batched in ONE sitting — beats daily anything.
- 1 carousel/week if time allows. Stories = reposts + polls, low effort.
- Every post: location tag, comment-bait caption, App Store link in bio.

## Rules

1. Instagram sells the **guide + planner** — never pitch Share to strangers
   (friends-only can't be marketed; it's the surprise inside).
2. Content comes FROM the dataset — if a post needs research the app doesn't
   contain, it's the wrong post.
3. "What to order" captions on food posts — same voice as the app.
4. Track one number weekly: **installs from bio link** (App Store Connect →
   sources). Followers are vanity until Stage 2.
