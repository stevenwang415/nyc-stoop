# NYC Stoop — marketing handoff brief

For any agent/chat helping with Instagram marketing. Self-contained: assume
zero prior context. Owner: Steven Wang (solo developer, lives in NYC).

## The product

**NYC Stoop** — iOS app (live on the App Store, v1.0; v2.0 imminent). A
curated NYC guide + trip planner built by one person. Not a review
aggregator: ~870 hand-curated places with editorial descriptions and
insider "what to order" tips, plus live events.

The differentiator: **routed day plans.** Pick places → the app builds a
day-by-day itinerary grouped by neighborhood, with subway directions
door-to-door, times per stop, and one-tap handoff to Apple/Google Maps.
No other NYC account or app shows this.

v2.0 (days away) adds: a redesigned UI, map with nearest-places list, and a
friends-only photo-sharing layer ("Your Stoop" — your photo map of NYC).
**RULE: never market the social feature to strangers** — it's friends-only
by design; Instagram sells the guide + planner. Share is the surprise inside.

Monetization: free app; $3.99 lifetime unlock (in progress). Instagram's job
is installs now, sponsorship-ready audience later.

## Brand voice & look

- Voice: a smart friend who lives here. Warm, direct, specific. Never
  listicle-hype ("HIDDEN GEMS!!"), never corporate. Say the dish, the time
  to go, the train to take.
- The "built by one person" story is an asset — use it.
- Visual identity (for graphics): warm paper background #FBF8F3, ink text
  #17130F, vermilion accent #C8321A (actions/highlights only), amber
  #F0A32B for time/live labels. Display font: Bricolage Grotesque (bold,
  tight tracking). Body: Instrument Sans. Brand motifs: squared top-left
  corner on photos; the "Stoop mark" (brownstone doorway + 3 steps).

## Strategy (agreed with Steven — docs/INSTAGRAM_PLAYBOOK.md has the full page)

1. Content comes FROM the app's dataset. If a post needs outside research,
   it's the wrong post.
2. Formats by leverage: routed-day Reels > "3 spots locals go" Reels >
   app screen-records > day-plan carousels.
3. Growth loop: caption "Comment DUMBO and I'll send the route" → auto-DM
   (ManyChat) replies with route + App Store link.
4. Cadence: 3 Reels/week, batched. Always location-tag.
5. KPI: installs from bio link (not follower count).

## First-post assignment

Design the opening 9-grid + the first Reel:
- Grid mix: 3 routed-day posts, 3 "spots" posts, 3 app/brand posts.
- First Reel: a routed Saturday (e.g., Dumbo or West Village) — hook in
  first 1.5s, show the map routing, 3–4 real stops with names + one tip
  each, end on "full route in the app — link in bio" + comment-bait.
- Ask Steven for: screen recordings from the app, his photos of specific
  places (he has a 20–30 photo curated set in progress), and which
  neighborhood to feature first.
